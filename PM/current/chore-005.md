# chore-005: Simplify dev publish confirmation

## Status

Active. Revises the dev-publish checkpoint policy after chore-004.

## Scope

Replace per-item promotion, demotion, and evidence prompts with one default-No
confirmation before publishing to dev. A successful publish leaves every item
in `release-candidate-dev/`; publishing does not change PM lifecycle state.

## Acceptance criteria

- `dev-publish.ps1` asks once whether to continue with publishing to dev.
- Yes authorizes the publish; No, empty input, or invalid input cancels before
  changing PM files, versioned assets, commits, or remote branches.
- Do not ask for per-item verification evidence or demotion feedback, and do
  not move or append to candidate PM items during a dev publish.
- Update `PM/workflow.md` and `README.md` to describe the confirmation and
  unchanged candidate lifecycle.
- Preserve clean-tree, dev-branch, build, version-stamp, `-WhatIfMode`, and
  `-NoPush` safeguards. Verify confirmation/cancellation and candidate
  preservation without running a live publish.

## Constraints

- This changes dev-deploy authorization only; it does not automatically promote
  or demote candidates.
- Do not commit or push a dev publish as part of this work.
